## Detailed Failure Analysis & Diagnostic Reading

The blind test achieved **18 / 24 (75.0%) top-5 inclusion** and **15 / 24 (62.5%) exact #1 match** without any artificial weight tuning. Six personas placed outside the top picks for clear, interpretable structural reasons:

### 1. Story 2: Priya (Intended: `cybersecurity` | Actual: `game-developer`, `engineer`, `ai-ml-engineer`, `blockchain-developer`)
- **Diagnostic Reading:** In Stage 1, Priya's answers favored general computer systems (`q1_opt1`, `q2_opt6`, `q6_opt1`). While she targeted cybersecurity options in Stage 2, the `tech` question bank distributes substantial secondary weights across related software roles (`engineer` and `game-developer` share low-level C++/network protocols). Because `game-developer` and `engineer` have high baseline reachability in `tech`, Priya's score placed `cybersecurity` at rank 6 (just outside the 4-pick cutoff for single-domain sets).

### 2. Story 4: Ananya (Intended: `nutritionist` | Actual: `doctor`, `pharmacist`, `physiotherapist`, `dentist`)
- **Diagnostic Reading:** In Healthcare, Stage 1 questions emphasizing clinics, wellness, and medical health camps (`q2_opt1`, `q3_opt1`) heavily boost the clinical core (`doctor` and `pharmacist`). In Stage 2, while Ananya selected dietary options, `doctor` and `pharmacist` accumulated cumulative in-domain fit from shared therapeutic options, ranking `nutritionist` at rank 5/6 (overall rank 6, matchPct 62%).

### 3. Story 5: Kabir (Intended: `fitness-trainer` | Actual: `teacher`, `social-worker`, `ed-tech`, `doctor`)
- **Diagnostic Reading:** Kabir's student story emphasized coaching, mentoring younger athletes, and instructing form. When answering Stage 1 blindly, options like `q3_opt5` ("teaching emergency survival workshops and reassuring worried families") and `q6_opt6` ("gather affected people to learn collaboratively") pointed strongly toward the `education_social` domain rather than `healthcare`. Consequently, Stage 1 routed him to Education & Social Work, where `teacher` and `social-worker` swept top picks. This demonstrates that students who frame fitness as coaching/teaching will naturally receive educational leadership recommendations.

### 4. Story 12: Sameer (Intended: `film-director` | Actual: `content-creator`, `designer`, `journalist`, `photographer`)
- **Diagnostic Reading:** Sameer answered Stage 1 with creative banners and visual storytelling (`q1_opt2`, `q2_opt5`, `q5_opt3`). This produced a blended Stage 1 between `media` and `creative`. In the blended served set, `content-creator` and `designer` dominated the top 2 slots due to high dual-domain affinity, pushing `film-director` to rank 6.

### 5. Story 13: Nisha (Intended: `journalist` | Actual: `content-creator`, `film-director`, `photographer`, `public-relations`)
- **Diagnostic Reading:** In the `media` bank, all 5 roadmaps are closely clustered. Nisha's answers gave `content-creator` top honors (91%) because content creation encompasses digital publishing, investigative articles, and video reporting. `journalist` remained in the top-tier cluster but finished at rank 5 behind visual/PR branches.

### 6. Story 15: Meera (Intended: `civil-engineer` | Actual: `mechanical-engineer`, `cybersecurity`, `cloud-architect`, `blockchain-developer`)
- **Diagnostic Reading:** Engineering is a 2-roadmap domain (`mechanical-engineer`, `civil-engineer`). Meera selected machinery/structural options in Stage 1 (`q1_opt6`), creating a tie in domain affinity. In Stage 2, `mechanical-engineer` took #1 (77%). Under the Step 4/5 contract, picks 2-4 in small domains feature explore/adjacent paths to guarantee breadth across >= 3 domains. As a result, cross-domain explore picks (`cybersecurity`, `cloud-architect`) filled slots 2-4, while `civil-engineer` ranked in the 8-roadmap shortlist at rank 6.
